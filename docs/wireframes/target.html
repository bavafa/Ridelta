How to read this

"Likely riders" picks people who ride often. Many of them ride with or without an offer. "Moved by offer" picks riders whose choice the offer changes.

What this does not show

Long-term effects, repeat offers and driver supply. Prove covers supply.

Advanced: technical namesShow

The chart is an uplift curve. "Moved by offer" is uplift targeting. "Likely riders" is propensity targeting. "Everyone" is a blanket offer. Extra rides are incremental rides. Return per offer dollar is incremental contribution per incentive dollar. How many riders get it is the targeting cutoff.
