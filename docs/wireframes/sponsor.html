How payment works

  1. The rider sees the offer and takes a discounted ride to the store.
  2. The platform covers the discount when the ride ends.
  3. At checkout, the rider shows the offer code or a loyalty card.
  4. Only then does the sponsor pay its share of the discount.

No purchase, no sponsor payment. The platform keeps the full cost of that ride.

Mall operator: the operator pays. A purchase at any store in the mall counts.
