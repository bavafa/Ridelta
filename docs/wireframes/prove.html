Why the tests disagree

In a group split test, both groups share the same cars. Riders with the offer take cars from riders without it. That makes the offer look stronger than it is. An hour-by-hour test turns the offer on for a whole area for one time slot, then off. The two groups never compete for the same cars.

The trade-off

The hour-by-hour test lands closer on average but is less certain. Shorter time slots and smaller areas give more data, but the effect can spill over.
