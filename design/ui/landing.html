A portfolio project in marketplace analytics

More rides from every incentive dollar.

A ride marketplace gives riders offers to book more rides. Some riders change their plans because of an offer. Others would ride anyway. Ridelta shows how to aim offers at the first group, share the cost with stores and prove the result.
