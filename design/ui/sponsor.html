How payment works

  1. The rider sees the offer and books a ride to the store.
  2. At the store, the rider taps "I am here" within 15 minutes of arriving.
  3. The app confirms the visit and adds the discount to the rider's ride credit.
  4. The sponsor pays a fixed fee for each confirmed visit, whether or not the rider buys.

Each ride counts once. Visits are checked against the ride, the time and the phone location.

A visit does not mean the rider bought something. It also does not mean the rider would have stayed home without the offer.

Advanced: how visits are confirmed

A visit counts when four things match: the ride, the time since arrival, the phone location at the store, and a single check-in per ride. Where location is not accurate enough, such as inside malls, the rider scans a code at the entrance instead. The code changes regularly, so a photo of it cannot be reused.

Advanced: technical names

Starting areas are origin zones. Extra visits are incremental visits. Platform cost after sponsor fees is net platform cost. Fee per confirmed visit is a cost-per-visit price. How to check this for real is a holdout test with matched control zones. Smallest change it can spot is the minimum detectable effect.
